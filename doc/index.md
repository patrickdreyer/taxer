Welcome to the **Swiss Crypto Tax Helper**. This web application is built to simplify the annual tax declaration for private cryptocurrency investors in Switzerland.

Manually tracking transactions across multiple exchanges, wallets, and blockchains to comply with Swiss tax law is a complex, error-prone, and time-consuming task. This tool automates the process through a user-friendly web interface.

The application's backend securely connects to your data sources, aggregates all your transaction data, and applies the correct valuation rules. To ensure maximum compliance, it prioritizes the official year-end exchange rates (Kurswerte) published by the Swiss Federal Tax Administration (ESTV).

The final output is a clean and simple report, which can be viewed directly in your browser or downloaded as a CSV file. It contains all the necessary figures for your wealth tax (Vermögenssteuer) and income tax (Einkommenssteuer), structured for easy transfer into the official Wertschriftenverzeichnis.

This documentation will guide you through the configuration and usage of the application.
